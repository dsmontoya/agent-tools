# Product Requirements Document: Billing Plans

## 2. Product Overview

Customers choose between three billing plans during onboarding and can upgrade at any time.

## 3. Goals and Objectives

### 3.2 Objectives and Key Results (OKR)

Lift paid-plan conversion from 5% to 8% within two quarters.

## 5. Target Users

Customers on the free plan who hit usage limits.

## 6. Product Capabilities

The system surfaces plan limits in context, offers a clear upgrade path, and processes the payment method change without interrupting work.
